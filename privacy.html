Privacy Policy

Crypto Made Clear · Last updated: September 12, 2026

Crypto Made Clear (“we”, “our”) publishes educational short-form videos about cryptocurrency concepts. This policy describes how our publishing tools handle information when you authorize our TikTok integration.

What we access

When you connect a TikTok account to our desktop publishing tools, we receive an access token and basic profile identifiers (such as open_id / username) needed to upload videos you choose to publish. We request only the scopes required to post content you explicitly initiate.

How we use it

Tokens and identifiers are stored locally on the operator’s machine for authentication and are used solely to upload and manage Crypto Made Clear content on the connected TikTok account. We do not sell personal data. We do not use TikTok data for advertising profiles.

Retention

OAuth tokens remain until you disconnect the integration or revoke access in TikTok settings. You can revoke at any time in TikTok → Settings → Security → Apps and permissions.

Third parties

Video uploads are sent to TikTok under TikTok’s terms. We do not share your TikTok tokens with other third parties.
